# Andreas

I use Home Assistant and contribute fixes and additions to the open-source projects I rely on when i can. I am grateful to the maintainers who reviewed and accepted them.

## Contributions

- **[rtl_433](https://github.com/merbanan/rtl_433)**: a decoder for the Fine Offset / Ecowitt WH52 soil moisture, temperature and conductivity sensor ([#3602](https://github.com/merbanan/rtl_433/pull/3602)), with follow-ups for its battery and conductivity readings ([c21cfdb](https://github.com/merbanan/rtl_433/commit/c21cfdbe30e1cef2116f4b062018240febc3c250), [8fa6364](https://github.com/merbanan/rtl_433/commit/8fa6364c5c7e14665fe3d80d0553883ec14a4116)) and test signals in [rtl_433_tests](https://github.com/merbanan/rtl_433_tests) ([#508](https://github.com/merbanan/rtl_433_tests/pull/508)).
- **[ESPHome documentation](https://esphome.io/guides/diy/)**: an indoor air quality monitor added to the DIY guide ([#6590](https://github.com/esphome/esphome.io/pull/6590)).
- **[Mammotion-HA](https://github.com/mikey0000/Mammotion-HA)**: warning times shown in the mower's local time ([#902](https://github.com/mikey0000/Mammotion-HA/pull/902)), and far fewer device tracker writes while the mower is docked ([#941](https://github.com/mikey0000/Mammotion-HA/pull/941), [#949](https://github.com/mikey0000/Mammotion-HA/pull/949)).
- **[paprika-ha](https://github.com/rbrunt/paprika-ha)**: deleting a grocery item in Home Assistant now removes it from Paprika ([#92](https://github.com/rbrunt/paprika-ha/pull/92)).

Thank you to [merbanan](https://github.com/merbanan), [zuckschwerdt](https://github.com/zuckschwerdt), [jesserockz](https://github.com/jesserockz), [mikey0000](https://github.com/mikey0000) and [rbrunt](https://github.com/rbrunt) for their reviews and for maintaining these projects.
